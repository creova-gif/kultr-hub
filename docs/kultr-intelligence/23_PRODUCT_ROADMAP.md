## 2. Granular Stage Specifications

### Stage 1: Product Stabilization & Closed Pilot (Weeks 1–6)
- **Primary Objective:** Eliminate all P0 operational blockers and execute a flawless closed pilot in Nairobi.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Connect live Safaricom M-Pesa Daraja and Paystack merchant accounts.
  - Integrate automated M-Pesa B2C payout disbursement rail (`payouts.ts`).
  - Configure production APNs and FCM push notifications via Expo EAS.
  - Host 5 closed pilot events (500–1,500 attendees) in Nairobi with live door scanning and express drink lanes.
- **Success Criteria:** 100% ticket scan accuracy; zero overselling incidents; mean payout resolution time < 2 hours.

### Stage 2: Commercial Launch in Nairobi & London (Months 2–4)
- **Primary Objective:** Open public consumer registration across Kenya and the UK diaspora.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Launch Apple App Store and Google Play production builds.
  - Onboard 40 premier event promoters across Nairobi and London.
  - Implement automated 15% post-event escrow holdback for fraud protection.
  - Launch marketing campaign for "Kultr Canned Drinks & Express Bar Passes" at major partner venues.
- **Success Criteria:** $1.2M GMV processed; 35,000 tickets sold; 25% beverage bundle attachment rate.

### Stage 3: Regional Expansion to Lagos & Accra (Months 5–9)
- **Primary Objective:** Capture the West African creative hub and lead the "Detty December" festival season.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Deepen Paystack NGN/GHS direct bank transfer and USSD rails.
  - Launch semantic event search via `pgvector` for natural language discovery.
  - Release Creator Studio Mobile Pro with audience demographic segmentation.
  - Partner with 10 major diaspora travel festivals in Lagos, Accra, and Portugal.
- **Success Criteria:** 120,000 tickets processed; $4.5M GMV; 4,000 active KULTR PASS subscribers.

### Stage 4: Platform Scale & Creator Financial Services (Months 10–18)
- **Primary Objective:** Transform ticketing data into working capital liquidity for creators.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - **Kultr Capital (Working Capital Advances):** Offer automated cash flow advances to top-tier promoters based on historical ticketing velocity and sell-out track records.
  - **Dynamic Tier Pricing:** Machine-learning recommendations for ticket tier pricing and release schedules.
  - **Integrated Travel Packages:** Bundled flight, hotel, and festival pass packages for diaspora pilgrims visiting Africa.
- **Success Criteria:** $25M GMV processed; positive net operating profitability.

### Stage 5: The Global Operating System for Diaspora Culture (Years 2–4)
- **Primary Objective:** Unify live events, streaming discovery, physical beverage commerce, and cross-border creator settlements across all African and Caribbean cultural capitals.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Deep integration with CREOVA's Gopay cross-border wallet and Sauti-Os streaming infrastructure.
  - Enterprise white-label box office solutions for major stadiums and national carnival committees.
